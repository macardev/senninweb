Patient journey

How do international patients decide?

For a patient in London or Düsseldorf, treatment abroad is a major decision. Money, health and travel are all at stake. The decision can therefore take weeks and is made almost entirely online.

The 7 stages of the decision journey

  1. Awareness: It begins with a search such as “How much are dental implants in Turkey?” In their own language, on Google in their own country.
  2. Research: Forums, YouTube videos, comparison sites, Reddit and Facebook groups. They read about risks and bad experiences.
  3. Shortlist: Usually 3-5 clinics. Criteria include trust, the doctor, reviews, price transparency and ease of communication.
  4. Contact: They message several clinics at once, usually on WhatsApp. They send photographs and ask for prices.
  5. Evaluation: The clinic giving the quickest, clearest and most human response gains an advantage.
  6. Decision and travel: The appointment is confirmed once flights, accommodation, transfers and interpreters are clarified.
  7. After treatment: Follow-up, communication and a review. A satisfied patient becomes your strongest marketing channel.
Patients choose the clinic they trust most, not necessarily the best clinic. Trust is built online, in the patient's own language.
Where do clinics lose the most patients?
  • The site is only in Turkish or uses automatically translated English. Trust is lost in the first seconds.
  • They do not appear on Google in the target country; traffic goes to intermediary platforms and commission-based agencies.
  • WhatsApp messages go unanswered for hours or receive only a one-line price.
  • Prices, processes, doctors and guarantee information are unclear. The patient decides to ask later and never returns.

Market selection

Choosing the right target country

“Let's reach the whole world” is not a strategy. Budget, languages and operational capacity are limited. Being strong in 1-2 countries brings far more patients than being invisible in 10.

6 criteria for choosing a country

CriterionQuestion to ask
Price differenceIs the difference between treatment costs there and your price large enough to cover travel and remain attractive?
TransportAre there direct flights, and how long do they take? Short, affordable flights lower the decision barrier.
Language capacityDo you have, or will you have, a coordinator who can message patients and hold video calls in that language?
Diaspora and connectionsIs there a large Turkish community or an audience already familiar with Turkey, as in Germany, the Netherlands or Austria?
Existing patient dataWhich countries have patients already arrived from without deliberate targeting? This is often the strongest clue.
CompetitionFor that treatment and language, is Google's first page dominated by large platforms or clinic websites? Where is the gap?

Practical validation: a 30-minute test

  1. Enter the treatment in the target language in Google Trends, such as “Zahnimplantat Türkei” or “hair transplant turkey”, and compare interest by country.
  2. Choose the country and language in Google Ads Keyword Planner to see monthly search volume and cost per click. A high click cost suggests commercial value.
  3. Search in an incognito window on that country's Google, such as google.de. Who appears in the first 10 results? How good are their websites?
  4. Review your WhatsApp and form history: which country codes do enquiries come from?
Rule

Choose 1 primary country + 1 secondary country first. Add another language after establishing a first-page position in the primary market. Each language requires its own content, SEO and communication investment.

Legal foundations · Turkey

Legal foundations and trust

Healthcare falls within Google's “Your Money or Your Life” (YMYL) category and is closely scrutinised by search engines and the law. Two regulations in 2025 rewrote the rules in Turkey.

1. Authorisation and the 2025 regulation

The Regulation on International Health Tourism and Tourist Health dated 26 April 2025 (Article 5) states that healthcare facilities and intermediary organisations must obtain authorisation to operate in international health tourism. The same regulation requires complication insurance for surgical and interventional procedures in operating theatres and requires healthcare fees to comply with the Ministry's health tourism price tariff.

2. Promotion abroad (12 November 2025)

Key rules highlighted in TÜRSAB's announcement about the Regulation on Promotion and Information Activities in Healthcare Services:

  • Promotion must use a separate website or social media account aimed abroad, clearly stating that services are provided within health tourism.
  • The authorisation certificate must be published on the website or social media account.
  • Promotion that generates demand among residents of Turkey is prohibited; domestic audiences cannot be selected on social media.
  • Sponsored promotion aimed abroad, and announcements of discounts, campaigns and prices, may be made in languages other than Turkish.

3. KVKK and GDPR

Patient photographs, X-rays and health information are special-category personal data; GDPR also comes into play for patients from the EU. Include explicit consent and privacy information in forms and WhatsApp flows, and do not leave health data on personal phones.

Important note

For general information, not legal advice. Sources in the Turkish original: Official Gazette (26 April 2025, No. 32882; 12 November 2025, No. 33075), TÜRSAB announcement. Verify the current text with a healthcare law specialist before implementation. This chapter describes the Turkish context of the original guide.

Website

Your 24/7 adviser: a multilingual website

The 2025 promotion regulation made a separate website for overseas audiences effectively necessary in the Turkish context. Your site is your reception, brochure and first consultation at once. A ready-made template cannot do this job: trust comes from being distinctive.

Essential pages

Treatment pages

A separate page for each treatment: what it is, suitability, process, duration, recovery, FAQs and price range.

Doctor profiles

Education, specialty, experience, memberships, real photographs and, if possible, a short introduction video.

How we work

Online assessment → treatment plan → travel → treatment → check-up. Remove uncertainty.

Price transparency

Compliance with the Ministry tariff and clear package inclusions: hotel, transfers, interpreter. Visible authorisation.

Patient experiences

Compliant reviews, video testimonials and stories told in the patient's language.

Travel & accommodation

Transfers, hotel, interpreter and a city guide. Answer “What will I do when I get there?”

Technical foundations

  • A separate URL for each language: folders such as /en/ and /de/, with correct hreflang. Switching languages on a single URL does not rank well.
  • Human-edited translation: machine translation can be a draft, but a native speaker should edit the final text. Patients notice unnatural wording.
  • Mobile and speed: most patients research on phones. Pages should load in 2-3 seconds even on slow connections (Core Web Vitals).
  • Easy contact: visible WhatsApp buttons, a free online assessment form with photo upload, and a phone number with country code.
  • Measurement: track every WhatsApp click, form submission and call by country (Chapter 09).
Why not a ready-made template?

Dozens of clinics using the same theme look alike and seem ordinary. Custom design centres your clinic's story, doctors and target market's expectations. Without unnecessary code, it loads faster and supports SEO.

International SEO

Being found on Google in your target country

Advertising stops when the budget runs out. Organic SEO accumulates: built correctly, it brings more prospective patients to your site each month without paying for every click.

1. Find the words patients use

Patients search in their own words, not your clinical terminology. There are three search types in each language:

IntentExample (EN / DE)Which page?
Price“dental implants turkey cost” / “Zahnimplantat Türkei Kosten”Treatment + pricing
Trust“best hair transplant clinic istanbul reviews” / “Haartransplantation Türkei Erfahrungen”Patient experiences, doctor profile
Information“veneers vs crowns” / “Wie lange hält ein Zahnimplantat?”Guide / blog post

2. The right page structure

  • A separate page with original content for each treatment and language. Do not duplicate text by changing city names; Google penalises doorway pages.
  • Write titles and meta descriptions in each language using patients' search vocabulary.
  • Create logical internal links: implants → all-on-4 → pricing → patient experiences.

3. Credibility in medical content (E-E-A-T)

  • A doctor should write or review medical content. Include “Reviewed by Dr …” and an update date below the article.
  • Cite sources, avoid exaggeration and explain risks. Google rewards honesty in healthcare content.

4. Structured data (schema)

MedicalClinic, Physician, MedicalProcedure and FAQPage markup help Google and AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity understand your clinic. Patients increasingly ask AI as well as Google.

5. Google Business Profile

Complete your profile with an English description, real photos, the correct category and a service list. Ask international patients for reviews in their own languages; these can also appear in searches from your target country.

Authority

Building authority in the target country

Google judges trust largely by how other websites refer to yours. To rank in Germany, you need reputable German websites to mention you.

Sources of quality backlinks

Health tourism platforms

Profiles on clinic comparison and booking platforms can supply links and direct patients. Many operate on commission.

Local directories

Accurate, consistent listings in the country's health, travel and business directories.

Health and travel publications

Expert articles, interviews and guest contributions in the target language.

Diaspora and communities

Partnerships with Turkish associations, expatriate platforms and community websites in that country.

The budget reality

A single quality link from a reputable editorial site in the target country can often cost several hundred euros. The original guide describes €300-400 as common in markets such as Germany. Serious international SEO is therefore an ongoing monthly investment, not a one-off job.

DoAvoid
Relevant sites with genuine readers in the target countryBulk packages such as “1,000 backlinks for $50”
A few quality links, consistently each monthLink farms and unrelated private blog networks
Natural, varied anchor textUsing “hair transplant turkey” for every link
Content that adds value to the sourceComment spam and forum signatures

Important

Bad links do not improve rankings; a Google penalty can set you back for years. In healthcare, quality is always more valuable than volume.

Trust

Trust signals and social proof

Patients will entrust their health to you from thousands of kilometres away. Every page should convey: “These are real people who know their work.”

Real faces

Professional yet approachable photos of doctors and staff. Stock photography undermines trust.

Video

A 60-second welcome from the doctor, in the patient's language or with subtitles.

Reviews

Independent platforms such as Google and Trustpilot, in the patient's language. Check regulations before using reviews in promotion.

Credentials

Authorisation, accreditation, professional memberships and certificates.

Clinic tour

Sterile areas, equipment and waiting rooms convey hygiene and modern facilities.

Guarantees & follow-up

Clearly document aftercare and check-up arrangements.

A review collection system

  1. Ask about satisfaction 1-3 days after treatment, while the patient is still nearby.
  2. If satisfied, send a one-click Google review link on WhatsApp.
  3. After they return home, ask whether they can share a written or video experience. Obtain written permission.
  4. Respond to negative reviews within 24 hours, calmly and constructively. Future patients will read those responses.
A patient's experience told in their own language is more persuasive than your best advertising copy.

Conversion

The first 5 minutes: from enquiry to patient

Patients contact several clinics at once. Sales research shows that companies responding within the first hour are far more likely to qualify an enquiry (Harvard Business Review, “The Short Life of Online Sales Leads”, 2011). In health tourism, this window is measured in minutes.

A WhatsApp welcome flow

  1. Greet patients in their language and use their name. Use automated replies only outside working hours, stating when you will respond.
  2. Ask the right questions: concern or request, past treatments, available X-rays and photographs, and intended dates.
  3. Set an assessment expectation: “Our doctor will review your photographs and prepare a personalised plan within 24 hours.”
  4. Give a clear offer: treatment plan, price range, inclusions (hotel, transfers, interpreter), duration and required stay. Preferably as a PDF.
  5. Video call: a 10-15 minute call with the doctor or coordinator builds trust.
  6. Follow up: if there is no response, send polite, informative messages on days 2, 5 and 10. Instead of “Have you decided?”, try “We prepared a short video about recovery after implants.”

Measurement

What should you measure, and how?

You cannot grow what you do not measure. The aim is not website traffic alone, but knowing how many actual patients arrive from each country, page and channel.

The patient funnel and its metrics

StageMetricTool
VisibilityImpressions and average position in the target countryGoogle Search Console (country filter)
VisitOrganic visitors by country and languageGoogle Analytics 4
EnquiryWhatsApp clicks, forms and calls by country and pageGA4 events
Qualified enquiryPatients sending photos or informationTracking board / CRM
AppointmentConfirmed bookings and depositsCRM
RevenueRevenue per patient, cost per channelCRM + accounts

5 questions to ask each month

  1. Which pages are approaching the top 10 in the target country? Positions 11-20 are the quickest opportunities.
  2. Which page generates the most WhatsApp enquiries? Could we create a similar page for another treatment?
  3. What is the enquiry-to-appointment conversion rate? If low, the issue is in communication rather than traffic.
  4. How many minutes does our average first response take?
  5. How many new reviews did we receive this month?
Realistic expectations

Organic SEO is a marathon. Noticeable results in a new language and country typically take 4-6 months, longer for competitive treatments. The infrastructure built during that time - website, content, authority and reviews - lasts and continues to grow.

By specialty

Specialty-specific notes

Hair transplant centres
  • The most competitive area. Pages focused on techniques or concerns (FUE, DHI, female hair transplantation, beard transplants) deliver faster results than a generic “hair transplant turkey” page.
  • Who performs the procedure is the most important trust question. Explain the doctor's role clearly.
  • Results take 6-12 months to emerge, so long-term follow-up content and patient diaries are valuable.
Dental clinics
  • Separate pages for implants, all-on-4/all-on-6, zirconia crowns and smile design.
  • The most common questions are how long to stay and how many trips are required. Explain this with a clear timeline.
  • Implant brands and guarantee conditions build trust.
Aesthetic & beauty clinics
  • Advertising and before/after rules are especially sensitive. Use informative, compliant language.
  • Be transparent about practitioner qualifications, products, devices and recovery.
  • Holiday and care combinations involving short procedures with limited recovery time can make attractive packages.
Veterinary clinics
  • The overseas audience is different here: foreign residents in Turkey, tourists and people relocating with pets.
  • Explain pet passports, travel vaccinations, rabies antibody tests and health certificates in languages such as English and Russian.
  • Searches such as “English speaking vet + city” may have high intent and low competition.
Across specialties, the successful clinic answers the patient's greatest concern most clearly, in the patient's language.

A 90-day roadmap

This is a realistic starting schedule for a clinic beginning from scratch or with a weak digital presence.

PeriodActionsOutput

The 3 most common mistakes

  1. Trying to reach 5 languages and 10 countries at once without establishing depth anywhere.
  2. Commissioning a website and waiting for traffic to arrive. The site is the starting point, not the finish line.
  3. Failing to organise WhatsApp and follow-up when traffic arrives. This is the most expensive mistake: patients earned through hard work are lost.
